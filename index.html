Alumni Career Tracker

Salesforce · AI Agent · iMessage

1st place, JPMorgan Chase Code for Good 2026

Built over a weekend in Brooklyn for The Knowledge House, a Bronx nonprofit that trains young people from underserved communities for careers in tech. With Michelle Wang, Daniel Bershadsky, and Angelina Arasavelli.

  • A staff dashboard on top of their Salesforce org that flags alumni who've been out of work for 3+ months with no recent contact.
